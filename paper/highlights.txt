Highlights

- Over twenty forecast models compared on three test periods with ten seeds each.
- Persistence residual explains 1 h skill; solar-wind drivers explain 6–24 h skill.
- Physics-inspired delay and gates do not change accuracy significantly.
- With longer training, a residual Transformer matches boosted trees at 6–12 h.
- Forecasting changes, not levels, survives a factor-of-two satellite offset.
