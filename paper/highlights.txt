Highlights

- Twenty forecast models compared on three test periods, ten seeds and paired tests.
- Persistence residual explains 1 h skill; solar-wind drivers explain 6–24 h skill.
- Physics-inspired delay, gates and losses do not change accuracy significantly.
- An LSTM encoder makes the physics-guided model the most robust and transferable.
- Forecasting changes, not levels, survives a factor-of-two satellite offset.
